How the value is priced

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chain breadth. Every input comes from the snapshot itself — no protocol name is ever special-cased, and no figure is invented.

implied = TVL × catMultiple × (1 + clamp(7d, −60%, +120%) × 0.5) × (0.7 + min(age, 1095d) / 1095 × 0.45) × (1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 3))

What is excluded, and why

  • Exchanges — an exchange's market cap is not a DeFi multiple, and exchanges do not launch protocol tokens here.
  • Wrapped, staked and bridged assets (WBTC, staked-ETH receipts, bridge-wrapped BTC) — the asset already exists; there is no launch to rank.
  • Versioned pools (names ending in V2, v3, 1.1) — a pool of another protocol, not a protocol of its own.
  • Live-token protocols — ranked nowhere in the board; they appear only in the Live tokens view at their market value.
  • A protocol is called pre-token when its record shows no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. The snapshot records no token; this page never claims it has none.

Model · Implied value is a model of a hypothetical launch, not a price, a prediction or investment advice. Farm plans are a starting point: verify every step on the protocol's own pages before moving funds.
